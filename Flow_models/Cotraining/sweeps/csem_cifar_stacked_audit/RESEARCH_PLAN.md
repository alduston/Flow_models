# Scientific contrasts fixed before CIFAR results

The question is whether the practical FMNIST phenomenon transfers: naive joint stacked CSEM can produce good generation despite a biased factorwise teacher, and making supervision valid can improve measured score risk without improving finite-budget generation.

1. **Practical stacked benefit:** Compare full r=.5 and r=1 separately against optimized flat, within seed, at the final 800-epoch RK4-20/CFG-2.5 endpoint. Report both, including failures. CFG 1 tests whether guidance masks a deficit; CFG 3 provides continuity with FMNIST. Do not select the best checkpoint or retrospectively promote a winning guidance/solver setting.
2. **Interior pressure tradeoff:** Compare r=0,.5,1,4 within the same two-block architecture. Ask whether stronger pressure lowers naive loss/increases posterior noise while eventually worsening reconstruction/FID. Do not treat the known FMNIST r=.5 optimum as a predicted CIFAR optimum. This sparse grid can show a qualitative turn; it cannot locate a continuous optimum.
3. **Coupling and bias:** Conditional dependence trajectory, pathwise defect upper bound, and held-out signed witness lower bound answer different questions. A positive resolved witness lower bound detects bias in its conditioning mode. A nonpositive bound is inconclusive. Compare null and class paths because CFG combines them.
4. **Zero-defect control:** Compare full r=1 with decoupled r=1 at matched head and posterior modules. Competitive decoupled FID would show conditional coupling is not indispensable. It would not establish independence of aggregate latents.
5. **Fidelity versus generation:** At r=1 and r=4, compare analytic-corrected and DSM continuations to matched naive continuation, separately by seed. Require improved common valid risks before interpreting a FID loss as a risk/FID reversal. Include the unchanged source as a second control for generic continuation drift. Analytic and DSM agreement weakens an explanation based solely on teacher variance; it does not prove exact-score generation is worse.
6. **Sampling check:** Compare inherited RK4-20/25 and Heun-20 on the same sample count, label/noise banks, initialization and guidance. All stacked coordinates share one solve; one flat solve and one joint stacked solve use identical head architecture. This removes the old two-serial-solve inference-budget issue. Larger VAE cost remains.

Interpretation boundaries: two seeds are replication, not a precise population confidence interval; witness image SE is not seed uncertainty; raw regression loss is target/representation-dependent; fixed-encoder proper-risk improvements do not establish absolute population score accuracy. Exact score error over the full path and the causal sampler explanation are beyond this focused replication.

The optimized flat+B source is the baseline. If its absolute number fails to reproduce, distinguish a shared environment/evaluator shift from within-bundle paired contrasts before making historical claims. Do not reopen architecture or hyperparameter optimization during this first transfer test.
